# Welcome to My Cybersecurity Journey

Hi, I'm Yuri — an MSc Cybersecurity graduate from the University of Hertfordshire, currently working as a Flight Dispatcher at Luton Airport while building the skills and experience needed to transition into a cybersecurity career.

My goal is to become a SOC Analyst, with a long-term ambition of moving into penetration testing. This blog documents everything I'm learning and building along the way — honestly, including the mistakes.

---

## What You'll Find Here

- **Homelab builds** — setting up Proxmox, AdGuard, Wazuh, Zeek, OPNsense and more
- **Tool walkthroughs** — hands-on guides for tools used in real SOC environments
- **Investigation writeups** — documenting analysis and detection work
- **Certification journey** — notes and reflections from studying Network+, Security+, and HTB CDSA
- **Career insights** — what I'm learning about breaking into cybersecurity from a non-traditional background
